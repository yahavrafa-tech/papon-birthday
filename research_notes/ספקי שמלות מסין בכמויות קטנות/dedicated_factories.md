# Dedicated China-based dress / womenswear manufacturers accepting very low MOQ (1–10 pcs/style) for custom cut-and-sew

## Which China-based factories explicitly state MOQ of 1–10 pieces per style for custom-design dresses?

### Takeaway
Only two China-based factory sites found say outright that they will make 1 piece per style in custom cut-and-sew production: **Factory24-7 (Guangzhou)**, which lists dresses, cocktail dresses and maxi gowns, and **MMS Clothing (Chengdu)**, which is strongest in activewear. Nearly every other dedicated dress/womenswear factory site advertises "low MOQ" but publishes **30–100 pcs per style (often per color)** for custom work. "No MOQ" claims (Huasha Bridal, New Asia Garment) usually apply only to the factory's **existing/in-stock designs**, not to a designer's own patterns.

### Cited Findings

No USD sample price was found. — [OEM page](https://factory24-7.com/oem-clothing-manufacturer/); [ODM "No Minimums" page](https://factory24-7.com/odm-clothing-manufacturer-china/)

### Inferences
- A designer who wants 1–5 production pieces from her own patterns should shortlist **Factory24-7** (dress categories listed explicitly) and **MMS Clothing** (1 pc/style/color stated, but activewear-led). With every other factory, plan on paying **sample fees for 1–3 "samples"**, which in practice are the small run, or on negotiating Orange Sea's 20–30 pcs testing exception.
- "Low MOQ" on most Chinese factory sites in 2026 means **30–100 pcs/style**, often per color, so 3 colors × 50 = 150 pcs. Readers should treat "low MOQ" headlines as marketing unless a per-style-per-color number at or below 10 is printed.
- MMS's "1 piece per style per color" and Factory24-7's "test one piece" are the only statements found that meet the 1–5 pc requirement for new custom patterns. Both should still be confirmed in writing, including the unit price at qty 1–5.

- **Contact details** (emails, WhatsApp, WeChat) could not be extracted for any company because the pages could not be opened. Each site has a contact form or page at the URLs above.
- **Per-piece prices** for a custom dress at 1–5 or 30–50 pcs were not published by any of these factories. All say "quote on request".
- Szoneier, Modern Apparel and specific Humen and Shenzhen small-batch studios (named in the brief) returned no dress-specific low-MOQ data. Szoneier is primarily a fabric/bags/nylon-activewear maker (see fabric question).
- Years in business and certifications are mostly unverified. Hongyu explicitly says it is **not** GOTS/OCS certified (see fabric question). No BSCI/SEDEX/ISO certifications were confirmed for any listed factory.

## What do they charge for samples (pattern + sample) vs. small production runs? Concrete USD numbers.

### Takeaway
Hongyu Apparel is the only dedicated factory found that publishes **numeric** sample prices: **$50–80 for a cut-and-sew sample**, $20–25 for a blank, $50 for a logo sample. Others (Factory24-7, MMS, Tosinfashion, Orange Sea, HuiLin, Appareify) quote samples per style. Factory24-7 credits the sample fee against bulk. Sample lead times cluster at **5–14 days**, and bulk at **12–60 days**.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- For budgeting, a first custom dress sample from a mid-tier Dongguan or Guangzhou factory is probably **about $50–150** (Hongyu's $50–80 plus fabric, print and complexity). Development-heavy or occasion pieces could reach MMS's quoted $300–600+ industry range. This is an inference from the two published data points only.
- The number to ask every factory for is the **unit price at qty 1, 3 and 5** (sample-rate pricing) against qty 30–50. None publish it.

### Gaps
- No published per-piece production price for custom dresses at 1–5 or 30–50 pcs from any factory.
- No published pattern-making fee separate from the sample fee, and no grading fee, from any factory.
- Shipping or DHL costs for samples to Israel or elsewhere were not researched. Szoneier's $8 DHL figure is for fabric swatches only.

## Which ones can work with fabrics the designer picks (fabric library, custom print with designer's prints, customer-supplied fabric)?

### Takeaway
Most factories say MOQ and price "vary with fabric". Several explicitly handle custom prints and premium fabrics (Tosinfashion printed dresses; Sidi lace/silk/linen). **Szoneier** is a separate option: a fabric mill that supplies free or cheap swatches and color cards with 50 m/roll bulk, which a designer could buy and ship to a small-batch sewer. No source confirmed in writing that customer-supplied fabric is accepted at 1–5 pcs.

### Cited Findings
- Hongyu: "MOQ may vary with the fabric chosen". The finished price changes with the fabric used. **Not GOTS/OCS certified.** Certified programs start only at 5,000 finished pieces, and there are no certified samples on their own. — [Hongyu home](https://www.hongyuapparel.com/); [Sample Development](https://www.hongyuapparel.com/sample-development/); [Organic guide](https://www.hongyuapparel.com/organic-clothing-manufacturers/)

- Tosinfashion: dedicated custom-printed cotton dress OEM and floral-print dress pages (MOQ 50). — [Printed cotton dress](https://www.tosinfashion.com/2026/07/17/2026-custom-printed-cotton-dress-oem-tosinfashion-50pcs-moq-factory/); [Floral-print autumn dresses](https://www.tosinfashion.com/2026/09/07/2026-low-moq-floral-print-autumn-dresses-oem-tosinfashion-china-women-apparel-supplier/)
- Sidi Fashion works with lace, silk, linen and similar fabrics. — [sidifashion.com](https://sidifashion.com/)
- Szoneier Fabrics (fabric mill, also makes bags and nylon apparel):
  - Fabric bulk "starts at 50 meters/roll", 15% off at 200+ m, 25% off at 500+ m. Other pages give 500 m standard and 1,000 m custom, or 500 yards custom. Sample turnaround is 7–10 or 10–15 days. — [Szoneier Fabrics](https://szoneierfabrics.com/); [Custom Fabrics](https://szoneierfabrics.com/custom-fabrics/)

### Inferences
- For 1–5 pcs, the most workable fabric route is probably: **(a) choose from the factory's stocked or cut-length fabrics**, since buying a full roll for 3 dresses is wasteful, or **(b) buy short lengths and swatches yourself** (for example via Szoneier's sample program or a Guangzhou fabric market agent) and ship them to the sewing studio. Custom digital prints at sample scale exist (Szoneier's $10 print swatch), but bulk printed fabric runs from about 50 m.

### Gaps
- No factory page confirmed in the retrieved text that **customer-supplied fabric (CMT)** is accepted, or at what discount. This must be asked directly.
- No factory confirmed a browsable **fabric library or swatch book** for designers, apart from Szoneier's color card.
- Minimums for printing a designer's own artwork on fabric at small dress-factory scale were not found.

## Which are well-suited for a solo independent designer starting out (English-speaking account managers, help with tech packs, no setup fees)?

### Takeaway
The best fits on paper are **Factory24-7** (no minimum, 7-day samples, sample fee credited, dresses and gowns listed, multilingual site) and **Orange Sea** (startup-focused, 20–30 pc testing exception). **Tosinfashion** and **XUANCAI/Chengyuan** accept **sketches only**, not just tech packs, and do pattern development in house. MMS is the safest "1 pc" policy but is activewear-oriented.

### Cited Findings
- Factory24-7: no minimums; a test-one-piece option; sample in about 7 days; sample fee credited on bulk; JA, FR and RU contact pages suggest multilingual sales. — [Factory24-7 Services](https://factory24-7.com/services-clothing-manufacturing/); [JA contact](https://factory24-7.com/ja/contact-factory247-ja/); [RU FAQ](https://factory24-7.com/ru/faq-ru/)

- Qingdao Chengyuan Garment accepts "a fashion sketch, product photo, measurement information, tech pack, existing garment, or other reference materials", with sampling and revisions. — [chengyuanclothing.com](https://www.chengyuanclothing.com/)
- XinYuan accepts "a tech pack or a design picture". — [xinyuanfashion.com](https://xinyuanfashion.com/)
- Orange Sea markets to "startups and growing brands" and offers a 20–30 pc testing exception. — [Orange Sea](https://orangeseaclothing.com/)
- HuiLin has a dedicated "Low MOQ Clothing Manufacturer in China for Startups" page (30–50/style). — [HuiLin startups](https://www.huilinfashion.com/low-moq-clothing-manufacturer-china-for-startups/)

### Inferences
- A practical path for a solo designer: run **1–3 samples** with a sketch-friendly factory (Tosinfashion, Factory24-7, Chengyuan), then place **1–5 pc "production"** with Factory24-7 or MMS, or negotiate a 20–30 pc first run with Orange Sea.
- Many of the "best manufacturers in China" lists found (Appareify, Modaknits, MMS, Huasha, Siukid) are **written by manufacturers who rank themselves**, so their rankings should not be treated as independent.

### Gaps
- Explicit "no setup fee" or "no pattern fee" statements were not found for any factory.
- English-speaking account managers and tech-pack creation as a paid service could not be confirmed. The pages could not be opened directly.
